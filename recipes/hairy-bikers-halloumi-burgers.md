# Halloumi Burgers

**Chef:** Hairy Bikers  
**Serves 4**  
**Tags:** cheese, vegetarian, burger

## Ingredients

- 2 blocks of halloumi cheese
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Sea salt and black pepper
- Sauce: 200g Greek yoghurt
- Sauce: 1 tsp dried mint
- Sauce: 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Sauce: pinch of sugar
- 4 ciabatta buns, split
- 4 large lettuce leaves
- 4 tbsp chilli jam
- 4 slices of red onion
- 4 rings of green pepper
- 8 slices of cucumber, cut on the diagonal
- Mint leaves

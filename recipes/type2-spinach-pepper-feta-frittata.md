# Spinach, Pepper and Feta Frittata with Cucumber Salad

**Chef:** Type2  
**Serves 4 | Prep 10 mins | Cook 20 mins**  
**Tags:** egg, vegetarian, quick, type2

## Ingredients

- 8 large eggs
- 150g baby spinach
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 courgette, grated and squeezed dry
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 60g feta, crumbled (Limit)
- Small handful of fresh basil, chopped
- Sea salt and black pepper
- Salad: 1 cucumber, sliced
- Salad: 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
- Salad: 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Salad: 1 tsp red wine vinegar
- Salad: 1/2 tsp dried oregano

## Method

1. Preheat the grill to medium-high.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large ovenproof frying pan over a medium heat. Add the pepper and cook for 4 minutes, then add the courgette, spring onions and garlic and cook for 2 minutes more.
3. Add the spinach a handful at a time and stir until wilted.
4. Beat the eggs with the turmeric, basil, salt and pepper. Pour into the pan and gently move the veg so it is evenly spread.
5. Scatter over the feta and cook on the hob for 5-6 minutes until the base is set.
6. Put the pan under the grill for 3-5 minutes until the top is set and lightly golden.
7. While it cooks, toss the cucumber and tomatoes with the oil, vinegar, oregano and a pinch of salt.
8. Cut the frittata into wedges and serve with the salad.

**Eat:** eggs, spinach, bell pepper, courgette, cucumber, tomatoes, olive oil, garlic, turmeric, basil, oregano  
**Limit:** feta (regular cheese)

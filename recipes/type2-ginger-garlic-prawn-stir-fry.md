# Ginger Garlic Prawn and Veg Stir Fry with Brown Rice

**Chef:** Type2  
**Serves 4 | Prep 15 mins | Cook 25 mins**  
**Tags:** seafood, tofu, stir-fry, rice, fish-seafood, type2

## Ingredients

- 300g large raw king prawns, shelled and deveined (Limit)
- 200g firm tofu, cubed and patted dry
- 200g brown rice
- 1/2 head of broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1/4 white cabbage, finely shredded
- 2 carrots, cut into thin matchsticks
- 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 4cm piece fresh ginger, grated
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 red chilli, finely sliced (optional)
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds, toasted
- Black pepper

## Method

1. Cook the brown rice according to the packet instructions.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large frying pan over a high heat. Fry the tofu for 5-6 minutes until golden on all sides. Remove and set aside.
3. Add the prawns to the pan and cook for 2-3 minutes until pink. Remove and set aside with the tofu.
4. Add the remaining oil, then the ginger, garlic and chilli. Stir-fry for 30 seconds.
5. Add the broccoli and carrots with a splash of water and stir-fry for 3 minutes. Add the cabbage and pepper and stir-fry for another 2-3 minutes until just tender but still crunchy.
6. Return the tofu and prawns to the pan with the soy sauce, lime juice and spring onions. Toss for 1 minute until heated through. Season with black pepper.
7. Serve over the brown rice, topped with toasted pumpkin seeds.

**Eat:** tofu, broccoli, cabbage, carrots, bell pepper, brown rice, olive oil, pumpkin seeds, ginger, garlic, black pepper  
**Limit:** prawns (shellfish)

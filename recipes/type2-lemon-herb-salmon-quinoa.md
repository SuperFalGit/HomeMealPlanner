# Lemon Herb Salmon with Broccoli and Quinoa

**Chef:** Type2  
**Serves 4 | Prep 10 mins | Cook 25 mins**  
**Tags:** fish, oven, fish-seafood, type2

## Ingredients

- 4 skinless salmon fillets, about 120g each
- 200g quinoa, rinsed
- 1 large head of broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Small handful of fresh basil, torn
- 15g butter (Limit)
- Sea salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200C (180C fan).
2. Put the quinoa in a pan with 450ml water and a pinch of salt. Bring to the boil, cover and simmer for 15 minutes until the water is absorbed. Take off the heat and leave covered.
3. Toss the broccoli and pepper with 1 tbsp olive oil, half the garlic, salt and pepper. Spread on a large baking tray and roast for 10 minutes.
4. Mix the remaining oil and garlic with the lemon zest, half the lemon juice and the oregano. Brush over the salmon and season.
5. Push the veg to the sides of the tray, add the salmon and roast for 12-15 minutes until the fish flakes easily.
6. Stir the butter, remaining lemon juice and basil through the quinoa.
7. Serve the salmon on the quinoa with the roasted veg alongside.

**Eat:** salmon, quinoa, broccoli, bell pepper, olive oil, garlic, oregano, basil, black pepper  
**Limit:** butter

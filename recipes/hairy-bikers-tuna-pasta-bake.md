# Tuna Pasta Bake

**Chef:** Hairy Bikers  
**Tags:** fish, pasta, oven, fish-seafood

## Ingredients

- 400g short pasta
- Infused milk: 750ml milk
- Infused milk: 1 bay leaf
- Infused milk: 1 mace blade
- Infused milk: 2 cloves
- Infused milk: a few peppercorns
- Infused milk: 1 slice of onion
- Sauce: 30g butter
- Sauce: 1 onion, finely chopped
- Sauce: 30g plain flour
- Sauce: 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- Sauce: 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 x 150g cans of tuna, drained
- 250g sweetcorn kernels
- 100g Cheddar cheese, grated
- Sea salt and black pepper

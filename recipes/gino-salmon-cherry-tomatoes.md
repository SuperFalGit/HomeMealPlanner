# Salmon with Cherry Tomatoes

**Chef:** Gino D'Acampo  
**Serves 4**  
**Tags:** fish, oven, fish-seafood

## Ingredients

- 600g baby potatoes, halved if larger
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 250g red and yellow cherry tomatoes
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 60g pitted Taggiasca olives
- 1 tbsp capers in vinegar, drained
- 4 skinless salmon fillets, about 110g each
- Finely grated zest and juice of 1/2 large lemon
- 10 basil leaves
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

# Saffron Crusted Shrimp and Pesto Pasta

**Chef:** Navi  
**Tags:** seafood, pasta, fish-seafood

## Ingredients

- Pasta
- Shrimp
- Pesto (fresh basil leaves, garlic powder, salt, olive oil)
- Sun-dried tomatoes
- Saffron threads
- Garlic powder
- Salt and pepper
- Parmesan cheese
- Olive oil

## Method

1. Prepare the pesto: in a food processor, combine basil leaves, garlic powder, salt and 1/4 cup (60g) of olive oil. Process until smooth. Set aside.
2. Cook the pasta: bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and cook the pasta until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
3. Prepare the shrimp: heat 1 tbsp (15g) olive oil in a separate pan over medium-high heat. Cook the shrimp for 2-3 minutes per side until pink and cooked through. Set aside.
4. Make the saffron crust: mix saffron threads (soaked in hot water) with 1 tsp (5g) olive oil. Brush onto the shrimp, then coat with a pinch of Parmesan (if using).
5. Combine pasta and pesto: in a large bowl, toss cooked pasta, pesto and reserved pasta water until well coated.
6. Assemble: add the shrimp and chopped sun-dried tomatoes and toss together.
7. Serve immediately, topped with extra Parmesan if desired.

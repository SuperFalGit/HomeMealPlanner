# Turkey and Lentil Chilli with Brown Rice

**Chef:** Type2  
**Serves 4 | Prep 10 mins | Cook 40 mins**  
**Tags:** turkey, rice, one-pot, type2

## Ingredients

- 500g lean turkey mince
- 100g dried red lentils, rinsed
- 1 x 400g tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes (or to taste)
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 400ml vegetable or chicken stock
- 200g brown rice
- 40g Cheddar cheese, grated, to serve (Limit)
- Sea salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Add the onion, carrots and pepper and cook for 6-8 minutes until softened.
2. Add the garlic and turkey mince. Cook, breaking up the mince, until browned all over.
3. Stir in the cumin, paprika, chilli flakes and cinnamon and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the lentils, tomatoes and stock. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 25 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils are soft and the chilli has thickened. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.
5. Meanwhile, cook the brown rice according to the packet instructions (usually 25-30 minutes).
6. Stir the kidney beans into the chilli for the last 5 minutes and season to taste.
7. Serve the chilli over the rice with a small sprinkle of Cheddar.

**Eat:** turkey, lentils, beans, tomatoes, bell pepper, carrots, brown rice, olive oil, garlic, cinnamon  
**Limit:** Cheddar (regular cheese)

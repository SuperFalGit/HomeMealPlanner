# Masala Omelette

**Chef:** Nigella Lawson  
**Serves 1**  
**Tags:** egg, vegetarian, quick

## Ingredients

- 1 tsp vegetable oil
- 1 spring onion, finely sliced
- 1-2 chillies to taste, red or green
- 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
- 1/4 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 eggs, beaten
- Freshly chopped coriander
- Chapattis, to eat with

> Quantities are for one omelette - scale up for the household.

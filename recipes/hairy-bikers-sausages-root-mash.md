# Sausages with Root Mash

**Chef:** Hairy Bikers  
**Tags:** sausage

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 8-12 merguez sausages (depending on size)
- 1 large red onion, finely sliced
- 1 tbsp harissa paste (optional)
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- Zest of 1/2 lemon (CHECK: original reads '1/')
- 400g can of chopped tomatoes
- 200ml vegetable or chicken stock or water
- A few mint sprigs, to garnish
- Sea salt and black pepper
- Mash: 200g carrots, cut into 2cm dice
- Mash: 250g celeriac, cut into 3cm dice
- Mash: 250g floury potatoes, cut into 4cm dice
- Mash: 25g butter
- Mash: 1 tbsp finely chopped preserved lemon
- Mash: small bunch of parsley, finely chopped

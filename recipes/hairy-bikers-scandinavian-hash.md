# Scandinavian Hash

**Chef:** Hairy Bikers  
**Serves 4**  
**Tags:** egg, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 300g potatoes, unpeeled and diced
- 200g carrots, diced
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 300g mushrooms, roughly chopped
- 15g dried mushrooms, soaked in 50ml warm water
- 1 tsp juniper berries, crushed
- A few rasps of nutmeg
- Sea salt and black pepper
- Sauce: 1 tbsp olive oil
- Sauce: 25g butter
- Sauce: 1 shallot, finely chopped
- Sauce: 200g mushrooms, very finely chopped
- Sauce: 1 thyme sprig
- Sauce: 1 tbsp plain flour
- Sauce: 250ml mushroom stock
- Sauce: 1 tbsp mushroom ketchup
- Sauce: 100g yoghurt or crème fraîche (optional)
- 4 fried eggs
- Lingonberry jam (optional)

# Spiced Chicken and Chickpea Tray Bake

**Chef:** Type2  
**Serves 4 | Prep 15 mins | Cook 35 mins**  
**Tags:** chicken, tray-bake, oven, type2

## Ingredients

- 4 skinless chicken breasts, about 130g each, cut into large chunks
- 1 x 400g tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 bell peppers (mixed colours), cut into chunks
- 2 courgettes, cut into half-moons
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 250g cherry tomatoes
- 1 medium sweet potato, cut into 2cm cubes (Limit)
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2cm piece fresh ginger, grated
- 150g low-fat Greek yoghurt, to serve
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- Sea salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200C (180C fan).
2. Mix the olive oil, turmeric, cinnamon, cumin, garlic, ginger, salt and pepper in a large bowl.
3. Add the chicken, chickpeas, peppers, courgettes, onion and sweet potato. Toss well to coat everything.
4. Spread over one large or two medium trays in a single layer. Roast for 20 minutes.
5. Add the cherry tomatoes, give everything a turn and roast for a further 12-15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sweet potato is tender.
6. Stir the lemon juice and a pinch of salt into the yoghurt.
7. Serve the tray bake with a spoonful of the lemon yoghurt on top.

**Eat:** skinless chicken, chickpeas, bell peppers, courgette (zucchini), tomatoes, Greek yoghurt, olive oil, turmeric, cinnamon, garlic, ginger  
**Limit:** sweet potato
